WHO WE ARE

Taranis Racing is a five-strong Irish STEM Racing team who have qualified for the National Finals in Kinsale, Season 2025/26. Named after Taranis — the Celtic deity of thunder and lightning — we are driven by a shared ambition to be as fierce as thunder and as fast as lightning in this thrilling competition.
